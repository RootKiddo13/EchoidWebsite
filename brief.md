# Echoid Website — Product Brief

## Purpose

Create a compact Turkish-language companion website for Echoid. The page should introduce the channel and offer direct paths to its YouTube channel, X profile, projects on GitHub, and contact email.

## Product principles

- Echoid has its own Turkish voice and identity; this is not a localized RootKiddo clone.
- Use the user-approved Echoid hero artwork and the canonical matte, flat-shaded 3D character design.
- Keep the first version focused, quick to scan, responsive, and accessible.
- Keep channel data and destinations in one typed data source.
- Use a static site unless a later approved requirement changes the scope.

## First-version scope

- One home page with Echoid identity, short introduction, and the four confirmed destinations.
- A concise About interaction, following the final approved design.
- Responsive layout, keyboard access, and reduced-motion support.
- Static asset and production build checks.

## Out of scope

- YouTube API, live video feed, CMS, database, accounts, analytics, or forms.
- Automatic publishing, hosting, or domain configuration.
- Unreviewed copy, artwork, or effects may appear only as clearly marked preview proposals and must not be treated as publication-ready.

## Confirmed destinations

See const.md for the authoritative YouTube, X, GitHub, and Contact values.
